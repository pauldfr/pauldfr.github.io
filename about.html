About me

My work connects questions about human judgment and values with the practical task of evaluating AI systems.

Building independent evaluation

I am the co-founder and executive director of Sophron Research, an independent nonprofit developing evaluations of frontier AI systems. I lead organizational strategy and partnerships, have raised $2.2 million, and led a successful research scientist hiring process.

I co-created Pander Score, a regularly updated benchmark of AI sycophancy, and co-authored DeliberationBench, which examines AI influence on people’s views. I also mentor four researchers through SPAR and am a research affiliate with the MINT lab.

Philosophy and cognitive science

I received my PhD in Philosophy from the University of Michigan in 2026, alongside a Certificate in Cognitive Science. My dissertation, The Pursuit of Value, examines motivation through empirical and computational approaches.

From 2024 to 2026, I was a Department Visitor at New York University, working with David Chalmers. Before Michigan, I completed a BPhil in Philosophy with Distinction at Trinity College, Oxford, as a Clarendon Scholar, and a First Class BA in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics at Magdalen College, Oxford. My undergraduate thesis received the Anscombe Thesis Prize.

I remain interested in how preferences change, how reward relates to value, and what would count as evidence of consciousness in artificial systems.

Policy, fellowships, and leadership

At GovAI in Oxford, I researched AI’s implications for authoritarian governance and human rights. My report was submitted as expert testimony to the Tom Lantos Human Rights Commission, and I presented research to representatives of the Mexican government.

I have been an AI for Human Reasoning Fellow with the Future of Life Foundation, a Junior Fellow with the Institute for Humane Studies, and an Adam Smith Fellow with the Mercatus Center. My research has also received support from the Cosmos Institute, Longview Philanthropy, and Open Philanthropy.

Earlier, I founded and led Project Access in Sweden and served as president of the Oxford University Scandinavian Society.

Beyond the research

I grew up in Gävle, Sweden, known for the world’s largest straw goat. I write about mind, values, and related questions on The Internal World.

For research collaborations, policy discussions, or speaking invitations, contact paul@sophronresearch.org.
