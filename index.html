About

I am a researcher working on AI evaluation, safety, and governance, and the co-founder and executive director of Sophron Research. We develop independent evaluations of frontier AI systems, with a focus on how they affect human beliefs and decision-making.

My background is in philosophy and cognitive science. I received my PhD from the University of Michigan in 2026, and previously completed a BPhil in Philosophy and a BA in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics at Oxford. From 2024 to 2026, I was a Department Visitor at NYU, working with David Chalmers.

Research

My current work focuses on evaluating AI behavior and its implications for human agency and governance. Selected projects include:

  • Pander Score, a regularly updated sycophancy benchmark measuring how strongly AI models defer to users’ beliefs.
  • DeliberationBench, a benchmark comparing AI’s influence on users’ views with opinion changes following structured human deliberation.
  • AI risk prioritization, a collaborative Delphi study of 272 international experts on AI risks and mitigation priorities.

I also work on human values, motivation, decision theory, and consciousness. See my research page for publications and working papers.

Policy and public engagement

I previously worked at GovAI in Oxford on AI, authoritarian governance, and human rights. My report was submitted as expert testimony to the Tom Lantos Human Rights Commission, and I presented research to representatives of the Mexican government.

More recently, I advised on the AI workshops at the Institute for Humane Studies’ Liberal Solutions Summit and wrote in Svenska Dagbladet about human control of frontier AI.

Background

I am a mentor for four researchers through SPAR, a research affiliate with the MINT lab, and a former AI for Human Reasoning Fellow with the Future of Life Foundation. I have held fellowships with the Institute for Humane Studies and the Mercatus Center, and was a Clarendon Scholar at Oxford.

I grew up in Gävle, Sweden, known for the world’s largest straw goat. I write about mind and values on The Internal World.
