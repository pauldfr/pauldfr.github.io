About

I am a researcher working on AI evaluation, safety, and governance, and the co-founder and executive director of Sophron Research. We develop independent evaluations of frontier AI systems, with a focus on how they affect human beliefs and decision-making.

My background is in philosophy and cognitive science. I received my PhD from the University of Michigan in 2026, and previously completed a BPhil in Philosophy and a BA in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics at Oxford. From 2024 to 2026, I was a Department Visitor at NYU, working with David Chalmers.

Research

My current work focuses on evaluating AI behavior and its implications for human agency and governance. Selected projects include:

  • Pander Score, a regularly updated sycophancy benchmark measuring how strongly AI models defer to users’ beliefs.
  • DeliberationBench, a benchmark comparing AI’s influence on users’ views with opinion changes following structured human deliberation.
  • MoReBench, a benchmark evaluating procedural and pluralistic moral reasoning in language models, beyond their final answers.

I also work on human values, motivation, decision theory, and consciousness. See my research page for publications and working papers. I write about mind, values, and related questions on my Substack, The Internal World.

Recognition and engagement

My research has received an AI Alignment Awards final prize and an AI Risk Analysis Award, with publications in ICLR and Philosophy of Science and papers accepted to NeurIPS workshops.

I was a Clarendon Scholar at Oxford, where I received the Anscombe Thesis Prize for the best undergraduate philosophy thesis. I have held fellowships with the Institute for Humane Studies and the Mercatus Center, and participated in the AI for Human Reasoning Fellowship.

At GovAI, my research on AI and human rights was submitted as expert testimony to the Tom Lantos Human Rights Commission of the U.S. House of Representatives. More recently, I participated as an expert in the MIT AI risk prioritization survey. I also mentor AI safety researchers through SPAR.
